Montgomery County Commission minutes, 2020-02-10 to 2026-05-11

Data downloads

Every shown fact, with its source, as files: so this site is not the only copy. The files carry what the pages show, and none of the program's raw reading of names, vote wording or motion sentences.

Montgomery County Commission

  • facts.json: All shown facts and their sources, in one file.
  • officials.csv: One row per official.
  • meetings.csv: One row per meeting.
  • attendance.csv: Verified attendance, one row per member per meeting.
  • roll_calls.csv: One row per roll call, with whether it is confirmed.
  • votes.csv: One row per vote in a roll call.
  • motions.csv: One row per motion, with its kind.
  • items.csv: One row per agenda item.
  • item_events.csv: Each appearance of an item at a meeting.
  • sources.csv: Every source document, with its address and fetch date.
  • seats.csv: Seat holder records: first and last meetings seen in roll calls, or a term as its source prints it.

Clarksville City Council

  • city/facts.json: All shown facts and their sources, in one file.
  • city/officials.csv: One row per official.
  • city/meetings.csv: One row per meeting.
  • city/attendance.csv: Verified attendance, one row per member per meeting.
  • city/roll_calls.csv: One row per roll call, with whether it is confirmed.
  • city/votes.csv: One row per vote in a roll call.
  • city/motions.csv: One row per motion, with its kind.
  • city/items.csv: One row per agenda item.
  • city/item_events.csv: Each appearance of an item at a meeting.
  • city/sources.csv: Every source document, with its address and fetch date.
  • city/seats.csv: Seat holder records: first and last meetings seen in roll calls, or a term as its source prints it.

Bids, awards and budgets

  • finance.json: Bids and awards and the budget books, as the pages show them, with every source.
  • solicitations.csv: One row per solicitation in the purchasing archive.
  • bidders.csv: One row per bidder on a bid tab that was read, in the tab's printed order.
  • fin_documents.csv: Every bid and award document, with its address and fetch date.
  • budget_lines.csv: One row per budget line per column, as printed.
  • budget_checks.csv: Each subtotal and total line per column: the printed total, the sum of its lines and the difference.

Every row names the document that prints it (source_url, page) and when it was fetched. A value held back as "Not confirmed" is empty in these files, with the check that held it.

A text cell that would begin with =, +, - or @ (or a tab or carriage return) starts with an apostrophe, so a spreadsheet shows it as text; number columns are left as they are.

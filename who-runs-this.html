Who runs this

The operator

This site is run by gnoshcodes, a small software company. It is independent. It is not an official government site, and it is not run by, paid by or approved by the county, the city or any official, candidate, party or campaign.

Every record page is an unofficial copy of public records and links the official record. Our rules say how coverage is decided and what no one can buy. Method says how the records are collected and checked.

How this site is funded

The site is free to read. There are no advertisements. It is supported by tips through Buy Me a Coffee.

No payers yet. No person, company, office or group pays for this site or for what it covers. If one ever does, this page will list the payer's name, the amount and the contract, as our rules require.

The bid tab does not name an awardee.

The amount shown is the total printed for each bidder on the bid tab (for some tabs a base bid, or a total including alternates, as labelled there). It is not the value of any contract.

Notice of intent, award letter and commission approval are separate events. Dates are shown per document.

Words used on this page
Award letter
A letter from the county to the bidder it chose. Most are scanned images and are linked, not read.
Bid tab
A table listing each bidder and the total it bid. Short for bid tabulation.
Notice of intent
A notice that the county plans to award to a named bidder. It comes before an award letter.
RFP (request for proposals)
A document asking companies to propose how they would do work, and at what price.

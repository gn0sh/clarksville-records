    How the address lookup works

    The Census point is checked against the current district lines and the city ward lines, as traced for this site. District lines: County Districts 2021, Montgomery County Election Commission (redistricting map PDF) (opens in a new tab), fetched 2026-11-08. The map PDF prints Districts 19 and 20 the other way round; this site uses the numbers printed in the roll-call tables and the county's Elected Officials List (opens in a new tab) (p. 9). Ward lines: City Council Wards (36x36 poster), Clarksville-Montgomery County Regional Planning Commission, updated February 25, 2026 (opens in a new tab), fetched 2026-11-10. Old district lines: the map has not been found yet; county 2021 redistricting page (opens in a new tab).

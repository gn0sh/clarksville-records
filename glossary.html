Glossary

Actual
The amount the county's books show was received or spent in a finished fiscal year.
Adopted budget
The budget the commission passed at the start of a fiscal year.
Amended budget
The adopted budget after changes made during the year.
Award letter
A letter from the county to the bidder it chose. Most are scanned images and are linked, not read.
Bid tab
A table listing each bidder and the total it bid. Short for bid tabulation.
Capital project
A line the budget book lists under the Capital Projects Fund, shown under the heading the book uses. It is not the total cost of a project.
Fund
A separate set of accounts the county keeps for one purpose, with its own revenue and spending.
Notice of intent
A notice that the county plans to award to a named bidder. It comes before an award letter.
RFP (request for proposals)
A document asking companies to propose how they would do work, and at what price.

Daylight Digest

Your local government, in broad daylight.

A city council, a county commission and a school board meet near you, in public, and decide things like roads, zoning, schools and local taxes. Type your address to see who they are and what they take up next.

We never get your address and never sell anything.

Your address goes from your browser straight to the US Census Bureau, which finds your area. Daylight Digest never sees or keeps it. Census privacy policy

See what we cover first, without typing anything

How it works

  1. Type your address.It goes to the Census Bureau, not to us.
  2. Meet your local governments.City or town, county, township and school district, each marked covered, not covered yet or can't cover.
  3. See what's coming up.Their next meetings and agendas, in their own words, each linked to the official document.

Why look?

These meetings are where your street, your schools and your local tax bill get decided. The agendas are public records, and most meetings are open to anyone who walks in.

You don't have to speak. Knowing what's on the agenda is a good place to start.

Daylight Digest is run and paid for by gnoshcodes LLC, a small software company in Clarksville, Tennessee. It takes no ads and no political money. Who runs this

How this works, and what is saved

The Census Bureau answers with the names of the city or town, county, township and school districts for that spot. Only that list of governments is kept, and only on this device.

This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.

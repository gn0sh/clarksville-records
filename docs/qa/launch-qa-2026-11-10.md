## Bugs

### 1. `city-seats/current-holder-matches-latest-attendance` (blocker) - owner: backend-engineer
The most serious problem found. Every sitting council member is shown as a former member.
- Steps: open `city/index.html`.
- Actual: all 12 wards read "Current member not yet recorded". "Current" lists only Joe Pitts. The 13 sitting members
  are under "Served previously (33)". 14 member pages carry a seat range that stops early, for example
  `city/members/city-wanda-smith.html`: "Ward 6, seen in attendance lists 2016-01-07 to 2025-03-06".
- Expected: the same page's own attendance rows run to 2026-06-04 (facts: 13 members listed at each of 15 meetings after
  2025-03-06), and the heading says "Current: in the roll calls of the latest minutes read, 2026-02-05". Members with
  confirmed votes on 2026-02-05 should be current, with their ward.
- Also affected: `data/city/seats.csv` (seen_last 2025-03-06 on 13 rows), ward lookups on the landing map and "My two seats".
- Likely cause (not confirmed): the seat rows were derived before the 2025-04 to 2026-06 attendance was added.
- Not caught by site_checks or pytest: add a check that a member in the latest attendance list has a current seat.

### 2. `corrections/channel-set-in-production-build` (major, blocks plan section 8) - owner: manager (CEO picks the value), then devops
- Steps: build with `--target pages`; follow any "Report an error" link.
- Actual: `report.html` says "Corrections open when the site launches." and offers no way to send a report; the last line
  still says "The links above open GitHub or your email program". `config/site.yml` has `corrections.github_repo: ""` and `email: ""`.
- Expected: a prefilled issue form (R2) on the launch build.

### 3. `feeds/entry-date-is-local-day` (major) - owner: frontend-engineer (`sitegen/feeds.py`)
- Steps: open `feeds/county-meetings.xml`.
- Actual: every `updated` and `published` is midnight UTC, for example `2026-05-11T00:00:00Z` for the 2026-05-11 meeting.
  A reader in Central time shows May 10, 7:00 PM. All 41 feeds, 1,246 entries.
- Expected: the Clarksville date (local midnight with offset, or noon UTC).
- Related: the subtitle says entries appear "when it is added to this site", but the date is the meeting date
  (minutes fetched 2026-11-07 are dated May), so late-posted minutes sort far down a reader's list.

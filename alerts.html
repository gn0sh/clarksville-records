How to follow a feed

  1. Copy the link of a feed below: press and hold it (or right-click it) and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into a feed reader. On the web, Feedly or Inoreader: choose Follow or Add subscription and paste it. On iPhone, an app such as NetNewsWire: choose Add Feed and paste it.
  3. The reader checks the feed and lists each new entry, linked to its page here and to its source document.

We keep no list of followers and run no sign-up. Each feed holds the newest 50 entries.

Email alerts are planned.
